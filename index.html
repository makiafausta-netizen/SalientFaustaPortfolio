01 / ABOUT ME

Why I work in robotics.

I enjoy designing systems that need to think and act in the physical world. That means understanding the sensors, writing the software, testing the control logic, and figuring out what went wrong when the real robot behaves differently from the simulation.

Through research at Widener and an internship at SharkNinja, I've worked on autonomous mobile robots, robotic perception, embedded systems, sensor validation, and simulation. I'm especially interested in vehicle controls, intelligent robotics, and practical applications of AI.
